Product Requirements Document

Stay-N-Sleep Corporate Loyalty Program

Product Manager: Rachna Bhardwaj
 Product Type: Corporate travel accommodation marketplace
 Prototype: Happy-path functional prototype


1. Product Background

Stay-N-Sleep is a New York-based online marketplace for corporate travel accommodations and unique local experiences.

Only corporate users book accommodations through Stay-N-Sleep. Accommodation supply can come from:

  • Individual hosts
  • Hotels

Stay-N-Sleep already has more than 2 million accommodations and has served more than 20 million guests.

Company Mission

Build a lifelong community of travelers and empower every traveler to have meaningful experiences.

Company Vision

Connect people and broaden horizons through new experiences and cultures.


2. Existing Corporate Travel Capabilities

Stay-N-Sleep already provides corporate customers with:

  • Admin dashboards to book and manage company trips
  • Price alerts
  • Specialized corporate payment options
  • Company travel-expense management

These are existing platform capabilities and are not the primary feature being developed in this project.


3. Business Problem

Stay-N-Sleep has seen increasing demand from existing corporate customers for a loyalty program.

Company research indicates:

  • Existing customers are 50% more likely to try new products and experiences
  • Existing customers spend 31% more than new customers
  • Retaining existing customers is generally more cost-effective than acquiring new customers

The executive team identified an opportunity to increase repeat business and customer value by introducing a corporate loyalty program.


4. Product Goal

Build a company-level corporate loyalty program that rewards companies based on their annual booking spend with Stay-N-Sleep.

The program should:

  • Increase repeat corporate bookings
  • Increase annual booking spend
  • Improve corporate customer retention
  • Deliver measurable travel savings
  • Strengthen long-term corporate relationships


5. Primary Persona

Derek Shepherd — Corporate Travel Manager

Derek manages corporate travel for his company.

Responsibilities

  • Book accommodations for employees
  • Create group bookings
  • Manage corporate travel budgets
  • Review travel expenses
  • Manage upcoming company trips

Primary Need

Derek needs an efficient way to book accommodations for multiple employees while reducing travel costs through his company's loyalty benefits.


6. User Roles & Permissions

Corporate Travel Manager — Derek

Derek signs into Stay-N-Sleep using Company SSO.

He has permission to:

  • Access the company travel dashboard
  • View the company's loyalty tier
  • View year-to-date company booking spend
  • View loyalty savings
  • Create group bookings
  • View company bookings
  • Edit bookings
  • Cancel bookings

For this prototype, Edit and Cancel are visible but do not need to function.

Employee

Employees can sign in with their company credentials and:

  • View their own upcoming trip
  • View their itinerary

Employees cannot:

  • Create bookings
  • Edit bookings
  • Cancel bookings
  • View other employee trips
  • Access Derek's corporate dashboard

Hosts / Hotels

Individual hosts and hotels can provide accommodation inventory.

Host-side functionality is outside this prototype.


7. Account Model

The company owns the Stay-N-Sleep account.

The loyalty tier belongs to the company rather than Derek or individual employees.

Example:

Company Account → Gold Loyalty Status → Derek accesses it using his Corporate Travel Manager permissions.


8. Loyalty Program

Corporate loyalty status is determined by the company's annual booking spend on Stay-N-Sleep.

Loyalty Tiers

Tier

Internal Booking Discount

Silver

10%

Gold

15%

Platinum

20%

Exact annual-spend thresholds for each tier are not required for the prototype.

Important UX Rule

The loyalty dashboard should not show the discount percentage next to Silver, Gold, or Platinum.

Derek can see:

Gold Member

But not:

Gold Member — 15% discount

The actual financial value of the discount appears during booking.

Example:

Accommodation Price: $6,000
 Corporate Loyalty Savings: –$900
 Final Price: $5,100


9. Prototype Scenario

For the prototype:

Derek's company is an existing Gold loyalty member.

Derek needs to book accommodation for multiple employees traveling together on a business trip.

The prototype will demonstrate this happy path only.


10. Primary User Journey

Screen 1 — Company Login

Display:

Stay-N-Sleep Corporate Travel

Primary action:

Sign in with Company SSO

Authentication is simulated.


Screen 2 — Corporate Dashboard

After login, Derek sees:

  • Company name
  • Gold Member
  • Year-to-date company booking spend
  • Total loyalty savings
  • One existing upcoming company trip
  • Create Group Booking button

Existing booking should show:

  • Destination
  • Travel dates
  • Number of travelers
  • Property
  • Booking status

Also display:

  • Edit
  • Cancel

These buttons are visible but non-functional in this prototype.


11. Create Group Booking

When Derek selects Create Group Booking, the flow begins.

Step 1 — Trip Details

Derek enters:

  • Destination
  • Check-in date
  • Check-out date
  • Number of rooms

Do not include:

  • Purpose of travel
  • Room assignment
  • Traveler count at this stage


Step 2 — Add Employees

Derek manually enters the employees traveling.

For each employee:

  • Full name
  • Work email

Action:

  • Add Another Employee

Derek can add multiple employees.

There is no employee-directory integration in this prototype.


12. Search Accommodations

After entering the trip and employee information, Derek searches available properties.

Results should contain a mix of:

  • Individual-host properties
  • Hotels

Each property card should display:

  • Property image
  • Property name
  • Location
  • Rating
  • Key amenities
  • Price
  • Loyalty eligibility indicator

Example:

Corporate Loyalty Eligible


13. Property Details

When Derek selects a property, show:

  • Property images
  • Property name
  • Location
  • Rating
  • Amenities
  • Check-in / check-out
  • Number of rooms
  • Number of travelers
  • Original booking cost

Derek then continues to booking review.


14. Loyalty Discount Application

Stay-N-Sleep automatically recognizes the company's Gold loyalty status.

Derek does not:

  • Enter a loyalty number
  • Enter a coupon
  • Select a loyalty tier

The system automatically applies the company's loyalty benefit.


15. Price Summary

Show the financial benefit clearly.

Example:

Accommodation Total
 $6,000

Corporate Loyalty Savings
 –$900

Final Booking Total
 $5,100

Do not display:

15% Gold Discount

The value should be communicated through the actual dollar savings.


16. Booking Review

Before confirmation, Derek reviews:

Trip

  • Destination
  • Dates
  • Property
  • Rooms

Travelers

  • Employee names
  • Employee work emails

Pricing

  • Original booking amount
  • Corporate loyalty savings
  • Final booking amount

Primary CTA:

Confirm Booking


17. Booking Confirmation

After confirmation, display:

Booking Confirmed

Include:

  • Confirmation number
  • Property
  • Destination
  • Dates
  • Employee list
  • Number of rooms
  • Original price
  • Loyalty savings
  • Final amount

Primary CTA:

View Trip in Dashboard


18. Return to Dashboard

When Derek returns to the Corporate Dashboard, the newly created trip should now appear under:

Upcoming Trips

This completes the prototype happy path.


19. Prototype Functional Requirements

The following should actually work:

  • Company SSO simulation
  • Dashboard navigation
  • Create Group Booking
  • Trip-detail entry
  • Adding multiple employees manually
  • Accommodation search
  • Property selection
  • Loyalty discount calculation
  • Price summary
  • Booking review
  • Booking confirmation
  • Adding confirmed booking to dashboard


20. Visible but Non-Functional

These should appear in the interface but do not need functionality in Version 1:

  • Edit Booking
  • Cancel Booking

They represent functionality available in the broader product.


21. Out of Scope

Do not build:

  • Real SSO integration
  • Real payment processing
  • Real property APIs
  • Real employee-directory integration
  • Host management portal
  • Room assignment
  • Flight booking
  • Car-rental booking
  • Consumer/leisure bookings
  • Full customer-support system
  • Full expense-reporting engine
  • Cancellation/refund workflows
  • Error scenarios
  • Negative user flows


22. Original Broader Loyalty Solution

The broader product strategy still includes the original three areas:

1. Corporate Loyalty Discounts

Silver, Gold and Platinum loyalty tiers.

2. Enhanced Corporate Dashboard

Improved reporting, expenses and corporate travel visibility.

3. Priority Corporate Support

Enhanced support for corporate customers.

The prototype focuses primarily on Loyalty Discounts + Group Booking.


23. Product Trade-Off

For the first prototype, we intentionally prioritize:

Derek's group-booking and loyalty-savings journey

instead of fully building:

  • Reporting
  • Priority support
  • Edit flows
  • Cancellation flows
  • Employee experience
  • Host experience
  • Negative/error scenarios

This allows us to validate the core loyalty value proposition before expanding the prototype.


24. Product Success Metrics

If the real feature launched, I would measure:

  • Corporate Loyalty Adoption Rate
  • Corporate Customer Retention Rate
  • Repeat Corporate Booking Rate
  • Annual Booking Spend per Corporate Account
  • Average Corporate Booking Value
  • Loyalty Booking Conversion Rate
  • Corporate Customer Lifetime Value
  • Total Loyalty Savings Delivered

These are KPIs we would measure after launch, not results we are claiming from the prototype.


25. Prototype Design Direction

The prototype should be:

  • Desktop-first
  • Professional
  • Corporate travel focused
  • Modern travel-marketplace experience
  • Simple and clean
  • Visually polished enough for a PM portfolio

It should feel like:

Corporate travel marketplace + corporate dashboard

Not:

Consumer Airbnb clone

And not:

SaaS subscription software
